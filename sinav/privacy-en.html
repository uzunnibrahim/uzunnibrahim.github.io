Netle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Türkçe

Netle ("the app") is developed by İbrahim Uzun ("we").

In short: the tests you choose, your answers and your practice results stay on your device. We have no accounts and no servers. The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows a small ad banner if you allow it.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your chosen tests, answers, bookmarks and practice results are stored in small files inside the app, one per test. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Settings › Reset progress deletes a test's data; deleting the app deletes everything.

2. Country

To show the right tests, the app reads the country of your App Store account or your device's region setting. It does not ask for location permission, and this information does not leave your device.

3. Question packs

At most once a day the app checks this website (GitHub Pages) for a newer question pack and downloads it. No information about you is sent; as with any web request, GitHub technically sees your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version may show a small ad banner on the home and result screens. Before the first ad, the app asks for your permission; if you decline, the ad SDK is never started. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is also shown.

Ads are requested as non-personalised. The app does not ask for tracking permission and does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA). When an ad loads, Google receives technical data such as your IP address (from which approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. Your study data is never sent. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Netle Pro removes ads.

5. Purchases

Netle Pro subscriptions and the lifetime option are sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

6. Questions

U.S. citizenship questions come from the official USCIS civics test (public domain); answer choices and explanations are ours. Canadian, Australian and Life in the UK questions are original and based on the official study guides (Discover Canada; Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond; Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents). The app is not affiliated with USCIS, IRCC, the Department of Home Affairs, the Home Office, ÖSYM, MEB or any government agency.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: Netle Support.

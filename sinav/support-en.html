FAQ

Are these the real citizenship test questions? Yes. The questions are the official USCIS 2025 civics questions. In the real interview the officer asks up to 20 questions out loud and you need 12 correct answers; the app turns each question into multiple choice so you can practise. Questions about your own state, representative or current officials are not included — check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.

What about the Canadian citizenship test? The Canadian test has 20 multiple-choice questions and you need 15 correct answers. Our questions are original and based on the official study guide Discover Canada; read the guide as well.

Life in the UK and Australian citizenship tests? Life in the UK: 24 questions in 45 minutes, pass mark 18. Australian citizenship: 20 questions in 45 minutes, pass mark 15, and all five values questions must be right. Our questions are original and based on the official guides; read the guides as well.

Is the app affiliated with USCIS, IRCC, the Home Office or Home Affairs? No. It is an independent study tool.

What is free? 5 questions per topic and one mini test per day for each test. Pro unlocks every question and test, full practice tests and removes ads.

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Exam Prep Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone? In the app: Settings › Exam Prep Pro › Restore Purchases.
